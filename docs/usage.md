# Using breakdowns

Review the card normally. On a side chosen in Settings, each kanji appears once,
in the order it was first found in your Japanese fields.

- Click or tap an expandable kanji card to open its component tree.
- Use the smaller arrows to explore nested components independently.
- Use **+N** to reveal more readings or meanings, and **Less** to shorten the list.
- Radical highlights and position labels help you follow the source structure.
- Open **ⓘ** at the bottom-right for offline source credits and snapshot details.

Unavailable frequency or component meanings are simply omitted.

## Refresh breakdowns

| What you want to refresh | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| The note you are editing | Right-click a field in the standard editor > Regenerate Kanji Breakdown |
| Selected notes | Browse, select notes, then Notes > Regenerate Kanji Breakdown |
| All enabled configured note types | Tools > Auto Kanji Breakdown > Regenerate Breakdowns... |

Standard desktop editor saves can update breakdowns automatically when enabled.
Imports, some other add-ons and experimental editing paths can require a manual
refresh. Disabled or unconfigured note types are skipped by regeneration.

Bulk operations show progress and allow cancellation. Completed batches can be
undone. Completion feedback distinguishes updates, already-current notes and skipped
or protected content. A skipped note should be checked in Settings; do not erase
handwritten content just to bypass a warning.
